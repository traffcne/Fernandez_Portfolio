Why am I thinking about you when I know nothing about you?

Is it too late to get to know each other? Do I even want to get to know you, would you even want to know about me?

Maybe I like the idea of being loved more than I like what love actually is. I don't think I've ever loved anyone for that matter, not romantically anyway.

Do I even like the idea of loving someone? To be there for someone everyday for the rest of our lives? I don't mind helping my friends, I don't being there for them if I can, is that any different?

Maybe I'm too selfish. Maybe I don't see the value in the effort, maybe the effort is what makes it valuable, or worth it, in that regard.

Maybe I shouldn't think about what it would be like to love you, when I don't even know if I like you to begin with.

I think I'll sleep on it.
