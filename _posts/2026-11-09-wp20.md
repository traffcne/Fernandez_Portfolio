The heavenly veil fell from its place on high, its anchors rusted and falling apart.
The sun once hidden from the common folk revealed itself for all to bask in
and the secrets of the world were laid bare. Truth became a commodity.
This about my stupid fucking blinds that I can't open cause it falls off of the wall at the slightest tug.
