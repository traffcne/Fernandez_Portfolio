Where did the years go? Four of them to be exact.
Four years that I thought I'd never get through
suddenly all gone in an instant. All the memories
remain, echoes of people who I no longer know
and no longer know me.

People I miss, people I don't, people who may
not miss me at all. People I fell for, people who
who I made uncomfortable, people I wish I had
made a better effort to be in touch with. It's all
ancient history now.

Moments of joy, moments of laughter, moments
of sadness, moments of anger. Moments of
vulnerability, moments when and where we
simply were. Moments I'll never get back but
will always have.

Twona, Ladder, Everett, Stickers, Director,
Orange, Aria, Chi, Pat, Jemo, Suave, Cubes,
Kairos, Verz, Stylist, and Skyward. I'm sorry I wasn't
the best friend, or the best person. Thank you
for the memories, in another life I might hope to
return your kindness.

To everyone I've met, everyone I've shared
a moment with, thank you for the memories.
Each of you are forever etched into my soul,
indelible as you are fleeting. Among the lights
dotted across endless eternity, you shine the
brightest.

And as time marches on, and the journey continues,
I would have believed that these memories would have
weighed me down. Each step has only gotten lighter.
