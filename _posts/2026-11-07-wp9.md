There are days where I believe that I exist in the background.

I'm neither the first, second, nor tenth choice for most people.

Some days I like it, being free from troubles that come with standing out.

Some days I hate it, feeling forgotten and cast aside by the world.

Some days I feel as if my role could be taken by anyone, that I exist as part of a long line of replaceable actors, none more important than the other.

I have no desire to be replaced, I like it here, I think.

Some days all I desire is to be called on, considered, remembered.

Some days are most days. Being in the background betrays me, much like this one has betrayed you.
