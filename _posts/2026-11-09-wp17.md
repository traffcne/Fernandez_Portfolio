I've never been certain if my anger is justified because
at times I feel like and sound like a child having a tantrum.
Am I losing myself or becoming myself when I succumb to my base emotions?
Am I truly so spiteful, so hateful, so lacking in concern or compassion for others?
Am I peaceful? Am I vindictive? Am I certain? Am I uncertain? What is revealed about me when I am angry?
Who am I really, is it part of what makes me whole?
I often find no answers when I'm screaming, nor when I strike.
I don't want to be so angry, I don't like it, but maybe I just
have to accept it as part of me.
