I get on the tricycle, the seat is uncomfortable.

A girl sits next to me, maybe she's a woman, I have no idea what age she is.

She looks like she could be in senior high, or in college, or working in an office, or all these things at once, somehow.

Her hair smells nice.

We start moving out of the station, I already paid my fare, and her smells nice.

Even at this hour the traffic is still pretty bad, everyone's just trying to get home all at the same time evidently. Her hair smells nice.

We pass by an important government office, which would be more impressive if half of the politicians weren't useless. Wow her hair smells nice.

We turn into a tight alleyway, one I'm familiar with now since I've seen it so many times. Dogs are left outside sadly, and people are winding down for the day. Her hair still smells nice.

Keeping my grip on the back of the motorcycle is difficult, especially when we go downhill. It's way too steep for the length it goes on for. Okay seriously how does your hair still smell this nice at this hour.

Almost home, albeit I have some walking to do after. I'm really tired, I'd like to go to sleep.

Is it Palmolive, Head and Shoulders, Kerosene? Kerosene, I meant Keratin. I guess those shampoo ads weren't lying, her hair smells nice, really nice. How the fuck does it smell this nice?

"Para" I tell the driver, and I'm off. I start walking the rest of the way home.

I don't even remember what it smells like anymore.
