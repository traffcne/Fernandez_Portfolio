date: 2026-10-31

You and I are alike, though I hate to admit it.

Praised as prodigies in our youth, many believed we would bring about a revolution, leading to a brighter future.

Many believed we would bring about the dawn of new age.

Instead here we are, reduced to menial tasks, and blamed for the twilight our forefathers brought about themselves.

I wonder, were we better off as unborn sons, daughters, and children?

Would we have been worth more as fleeting euphoria spilled along the bedside?

Would we have been better off as projects left unfinished, not worthy enough to warrant the time, love, and care of our creators?

Do I despise you for who you are, for what you are? Do I despise my imperfect reflection, so similar yet so different? Do I despise you for what the world has turned us into?

Maybe in another life we're brothers, and our worries amounted to the sun setting on our day, and our beds beckoning us to sleep.

The sun rises for us in another life.
