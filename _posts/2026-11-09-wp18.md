date: 2026-11-09

I want to lash out, to bite the hands that feeds,
To dig my thumbs deep into another's skull.
I want to see blood flow, tears fall, I want retribution.
I want to sharpen my tongue on the molars of those who
would speak against me, and cut their tongues to resemble mine.
I'm tired of leaving things unsaid and being amicable, I would have
left a walk-in closet's worth of skeletons had I not been taught otherwise,
or so I would believe. How quaint, one prone to violence spews hollow
promises when they don't even know how capable they are.
Still I dream, of upending peace, to let my lasting mark be
harsh words that cut into another's soul and a trail
of bodies that would leave Oregon blushing.
I'm tired. Maybe I'll want something different tomorrow.
